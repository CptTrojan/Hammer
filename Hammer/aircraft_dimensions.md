# Aircraft Dimensions

## Final Specifications

| Dimension | mm | cm | inches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wingspan** | 900 mm | 90.0 cm | 35.4 in |
| **Fuselage Length** | 700 mm | 70.0 cm | 27.6 in |
| **Wing Root Chord** | 400 mm | 40.0 cm | 15.7 in |
| **Wing Tip Chord** | 150 mm | 15.0 cm | 5.9 in |
| **Nose Length** | 100 mm | 10.0 cm | 3.9 in |
| **Canard Root Chord** | 100 mm | 10.0 cm | 3.9 in |
| **Canard Tip Chord** | 100 mm | 10.0 cm | 3.9 in |

## Notes
- Canard is rectangular (root = tip = 100 mm)
- Fuselage body: 700 mm + Nose cone: 100 mm = **800 mm total length**
- Wing planform: tapered (root 400 mm → tip 150 mm)
- CG: 13 inches (330 mm) from tail reference → **430 mm from nose tip**
